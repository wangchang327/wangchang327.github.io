## Things I Like

*Classical music.* I play the piano regularly. These days, I am practicing Haydn's Sonata Hob. XVI:49, Schubert's Impromptu No. 2, D. 899, and Balakirev's *The Lark*. My favorite composers are {{< extlink "https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergei_Rachmaninoff" "Sergei Rachmaninoff" >}} and {{< extlink "https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maurice_Ravel" "Maurice Ravel" >}}.

*Languages.* I have recently become interested in language learning. I speak a little Spanish and am learning French. I am fluent in English and a native speaker of {{< extlink "https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wenzhounese" "Wenzhounese" >}} and Mandarin Chinese.

*Chemistry.* I took part in the Chemistry Olympiad in 2016 and 2017 and earned a gold medal at China's national final in 2017. I later coauthored a {{< extlink "https://cchobook.github.io/" "tutorial book in Chinese" >}} with my high school teacher. Published by Zhejiang University Press in 2023, it has sold more than 18000 copies. While I am no longer active in chemistry, I update the book each year and still know my way around the subject.

*Gaming.* I enjoy simulation games that call for plenty of planning and engineering, like {{< extlink "https://www.klei.com/games/oxygen-not-included" "Oxygen Not Included" >}} and {{< extlink "https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/games/cities-skylines/about" "Cities: Skylines" >}}, as well as ones that let me switch off, like {{< extlink "https://eurotrucksimulator2.com/" "Euro Truck Simulator 2" >}} and {{< extlink "https://americantrucksimulator.com/" "American Truck Simulator" >}}.
