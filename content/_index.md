<p>
    I am broadly interested in economics and computation and learning theory. My current focus is on the theoretical understanding of swap-regret minimization algorithms and calibration, and on applications of the theory to making markets more competitive, particularly in addressing {{< extlink "https://www.justice.gov/opa/speech/acting-deputy-assistant-attorney-general-criminal-enforcement-daniel-gladd-delivers" "algorithmic collusion" >}} and related legal questions.
</p>
